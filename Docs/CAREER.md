# Career implementation scope

The career catalog implements an eight-chapter challenge series on **eight distinct circuits** across the connected development road network, with three physical opponents per event. This is a bounded progression slice, not the complete Nova City campaign. The fictional crew names, leaders and dialogue in `Content/Data/Career/career.json` are original. When a story leader races, their stable rival ID occupies an existing opponent slot, so persistent rival history follows them across chapters without adding vehicles or changing the AI physics model. These are AI driver identities, not voiced or separately modeled character assets.

## Catalog and progression contract

`UADCareerSubsystem` owns immutable chapter and reputation-rank definitions. It loads `Content/Data/Career/career.json` once when the game instance initializes. The catalog has a version, ordered `chapters` and ordered `ranks`. Chapter IDs are stable save identifiers; new chapters must be appended or accompanied by an explicit save migration. Reordering or removing completed chapters intentionally makes progression unavailable instead of silently unlocking unrelated content.

Each chapter supplies a title, crew, leader, briefing, victory line, existing race ID, difficulty, winning prize, finishing stipend and winning REP. Every chapter resolves its own event through `Content/Data/Races/catalog.json`: Dockside, Foundry Shift, Iron Quay, Night Survey, Northfield Run, Glass Coast, Sable Ring and Afterdark Final. Difficulty follows the existing Easy/Normal/Hard race settings, with no new opponent stat multipliers or rubber-band assistance. The events use different routes and checkpoint layouts. They remain circuit events; there are no bespoke crew vehicles or hidden challenge conditions.

The current authored lines apply `STORY_DRAFT.md` to these eight event IDs. Dax Kerr leads and races the opening Iron Wolves chapter; Ivo Renn returns for Foundry Shift and Iron Quay, then steps forward at Sable Ring and the final; Sel Arden races Night Survey and Northfield Run and invites the player into the final. Reya Voss and Wren Ashby also occupy their chapter's existing AI entrant slots. Mara Venn is mentioned in the opening briefing as the mechanic who brings the player in. The story leads use the existing vehicle and AI presentation; voiced dialogue, bespoke character models and crew-specific driving logic remain future content.

The current chapter is the next entry after the saved `CompletedChapters` prefix. A first-place finish awards that chapter's `WinCredits` and `RepReward`, then appends its ID to that prefix. Places 2-4 award `FinishCredits`, grant no REP and leave the chapter available to retry. A DNF, aborted race or unfinished classification is ineligible. Finishing every chapter awards 8,000 REP in total, enough for all eight rank names from Unknown through Afterdark Champion. Completing the series leaves no active chapter; free driving and ordinary races remain separate from career rewards.

`ComputeReward(Profile, ChapterId, Place, OutReward, OutError)` only computes a proposed reward. It rejects unavailable catalogs, unknown or already completed chapters, completion holes or duplicates, invalid REP, places outside 1-4 and REP overflow. Its output is cleared on every failure. It does not trust or inspect a live race and does not write a profile.

Race results also calculate a cosmetic D/C/B/A/S/SS/SSS grade using the route's authored `parSeconds`, total finish time including penalties, recovery count and final place. A DNF receives D; only a recovery-free win at 95% of par or faster receives SSS. Grades do not modify credits, REP or unlocks. Photo captures made from a result include a JSON metadata sidecar, and replay/photo overlays show the cached event-grade label.

## Runtime integration

The implemented race/ownership path captures the active chapter ID, resolves its catalog race and prescribed difficulty, and takes place from the race manager's completed classification. Career rewards are proposed against the current profile and committed with credits, REP, chapter progress, counters, receipt and rival-memory updates in one ownership transaction. Duplicate receipts are rejected; on a failed write the live profile is preserved and the result remains available for retry. The UI presents the story/rank text with the result. Leaders reuse existing AI entrant slots; they do not add bespoke vehicles or character assets.

Career decisions are local single-player rules. Checksums and receipts are corruption/duplicate-payment protections, not online anti-cheat. Future competitive services must validate race results and currency on a server.

## Data error handling

The parser rejects missing or oversized catalogs, malformed JSON, unsupported versions, wrong JSON types, fractional or non-finite numeric values, unsupported difficulty, blank/oversized text, control characters, malformed/duplicate IDs, finish stipends larger than winning prizes, and unreachable highest ranks. The first rank must start at zero and later thresholds must increase strictly. Arrays are limited to 64 ranks or chapters and files to 128 KB. A failed reload preserves the previous valid catalog; a failed initial load reports an error and leaves career unavailable.

## Validation scope

Career automation covers the authored chapter sequence, recurring leaders in their race rosters, dynamic Ivo/Sel briefing and victory context, reward constraints and route-specific pars. The current 54-case Unreal suite also covers map zoom/pan projection, discovered-only fast travel, safe placement, pursuit gating, waypoint persistence, and the moving arrival camera sequence. The portable race-rule suite covers grade boundaries. These checks do not establish subjective difficulty balance or a full career playthrough. Remaining career transaction checks include malformed catalog fixtures, all eight sequential wins, a paid non-winning finish without advancement, DNF/cancellation, duplicate receipts, failed-save rollback/retry, save/relaunch progression, invalid completion order, and final-series completion.

## Remaining campaign work

District territories and unlocks, sprint/touge career rules, modeled and voiced characters, bespoke crew AI, authored dealership locations, skill REP combos, branching scenes, and the full narrative championship remain unfinished. Stable rival memory persists across recurring opponent IDs and adjusts story briefings and victory context. The opening arrival now has a real-time in-engine multi-shot camera sequence; it is not a prerecorded video or voiced character performance. The separate garage provides a three-car ownership and purchasing loop; the finale is currently a catalog-defined circuit event. The exploration director connects eight one-time discoveries to the shared ownership profile; these are not additional career races. The catalog structure can carry additional authored chapters when those playable systems and locations exist.
