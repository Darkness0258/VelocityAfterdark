# VELOCITY AFTERDARK — competitor review and product gaps

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-30  
**Scope:** source-backed prototype inventory plus selected official feature references. This is a development comparison, not a claim of feature parity or release readiness.

## Current build, stated plainly

This repository contains a working Unreal Engine 5.8.3 driving prototype. The current code has five fictional data-driven vehicles, nine race routes, eight career chapters, a connected road-map/minimap, road-snapped waypoint and landmark fast travel, a local garage/save loop, weather/wet-road response, limited physical traffic, police heat/pursuit, an in-engine multi-shot opening with a recorded voice clip, photo/replay tools, and an optional direct-connect multiplayer prototype. Source and automation reports define which parts have actually been checked; several systems still have deliberately narrow behavior. See [implementation scope](IMPLEMENTATION_QUEUE.md) and [validation evidence](VALIDATION.md).

The 2026-09-30 local prepared run passed all ten stages on one recorded Windows package hash, including source/data checks, 971 vehicle-math assertions, 401 race-rule checks, 62 Unreal cases (57 succeeded, five warnings), packaged cutscene/voice/camera smoke, viewport requests, a two-lap race, garage relaunch persistence, one loopback multiplayer client, and rainy driving/performance capture. The fixes validated here remove unintended automatic reverse selection at race start, correct recovery-fixture expectations, and keep traffic-signal collision proxies clear of race routes. Full evidence and exact package identity are in [`VALIDATION.md`](VALIDATION.md) and [`Artifacts/Consolidated/20260929T205322Z-f5964cc3/report.json`](../Artifacts/Consolidated/20260929T205322Z-f5964cc3/report.json). This does not verify every feature by hands-on play, native display/controller coverage, the entire streaming world, online conditions, or final art/audio quality. The current model, roads, buildings and lighting remain development art.

## Highest-value additions, in dependency order

1. **Replace blockout presentation with a real asset production pass.** Commission/create original hero cars with correct wheelbase, tire and suspension pivots, usable interiors, UVs, LODs, paint layers, glass, lights and collision. Build fewer complete assets first; expand the fleet only after handling and material checks. Author a readable Dockside block and recognizable landmarks before widening the city. This is the largest visible quality gap in current screenshots.
2. **Add a local event creator before any sharing service.** Let players choose a supported route or connected road path, event format, opponent count, weather/time, laps and traffic preset. Validate route reachability, checkpoint order and finish bounds with the existing race rules. Save versioned local events and provide a preview/test drive. Do not expose uploads until identity, moderation, compatibility, privacy and service operations exist.
3. **Deepen world traffic and police as tested gameplay.** Give intersections collision-safe signal placement, lane-aware routes, avoidance/recovery limits, pursuit search behavior and clear player escape rules. Add focused regressions for PIT, roadblocks and traffic interaction before adding spikes or helicopters.
4. **Improve competitive progression without rubber-band cheats.** Add route-specific AI evaluation, clean-race/skill objectives, rivals who learn from telemetry, event replay modifiers and transparent rewards. Keep AI movement on the same vehicle physics and scoring rules.
5. **Treat public multiplayer as a backend project.** First complete 2–4 player reliability under latency, packet loss, host migration/disconnect and race authority tests; then add authenticated profiles, ranked records, matchmaking, anti-cheat, moderation and privacy controls. The direct-connect smoke is not public online support.
6. **Make a release content and device matrix.** Record physical steering-wheel/controller testing, native-resolution GPU runs, weather/traffic soak, audio listening and memory/streaming coverage on named minimum and target hardware. The existing offscreen captures and short benchmarks are not those gates.

## Verification rule

An item is “implemented” only when its actual runtime path is connected. It is “verified” only for the specific automated, packaged, device or player test recorded in `VALIDATION.md`. The reference games demonstrate product opportunities; their online services, licensed assets, proprietary AI and years of authored content cannot be inferred from this source tree or replaced with placeholder menus.
